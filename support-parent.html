Welcome

Thank you for using EduPilot Parent. This page explains how to get help when you need it. For the fastest response, contact us by email and include a short description of the problem, the steps you followed, and your app version.

How to Use the App

  • Linking your child — your account is linked to your child by their teacher. If you cannot see your child, contact your child’s school.
  • Monitoring progress — use the Dashboard to view attendance, homework, exam results and the progress indicator.
  • Notifications — make sure notifications are enabled to receive attendance and result alerts for your child.
  • Updating your details — manage your name, email and phone number from your profile.

Reporting a Problem

To report a bug or an issue, please provide:

  • Your device type and operating system version.
  • The app version you are using.
  • A clear description of the issue and the steps to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.
